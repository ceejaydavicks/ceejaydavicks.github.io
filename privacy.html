1. What this site stores on your device

We do not run accounts, logins or payments, so we do not collect names, emails or payment details. The site stores two things locally in your browser:

  • Age confirmation — that you confirmed you are 18 or older. Kept for 30 days in localStorage (key ageVerifiedUntil), with a cookie (age_ok) as a fallback.
  • Theme preference — whether you chose dark or light mode (localStorage key theme).

2. Cookies

The only first-party cookie is the age-verification cookie described above. It is set with SameSite=Lax and expires after 30 days. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings; the age gate will then appear again on your next visit.

3. Advertising

Advertisements are clearly labelled and served by third-party advertising networks once you scroll near them. Those networks may set their own cookies or identifiers and are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not run pop-ups, pop-unders or automatic redirects.

4. Video hosting

Videos may be streamed from a third-party host. When a video loads, your browser contacts that host directly, which can see your IP address and user agent.

5. Analytics

No analytics or tracking scripts are installed. If analytics are added later, this section will be updated to name the provider and describe what it collects.

6. Your choices

  • Clear the age cookie or local storage to reset your choices.
  • Block third-party cookies or use a content blocker to limit advertising identifiers.
  • Use a private window if you do not want the theme preference remembered.

7. Children

This site is strictly for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has accessed the site, contact us so we can respond.
